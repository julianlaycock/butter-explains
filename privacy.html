Privacy

Hosting. This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs. There are no cookies, no analytics trackers and no advertising identifiers on this site.

Instagram messaging. If you comment a keyword on one of our Instagram posts, our app sends you the link to that episode's cheat sheet as a private reply. To do that we store your Instagram-scoped sender ID and the keyword you asked for, so your reply can be matched to your request. It is deleted the moment the link is delivered. We also keep the ID of any comment already answered for 30 days, so nobody is messaged twice. We store no names, no profile data, no comment text and no message content. Nothing is sold, shared or passed to any third party. The only service the app talks to is Meta's own API.

Deleting your data. Message @butter.explains on Instagram with the word DELETE. Everything tied to your account is removed within 7 days.
